# MARIGOLD — Release Archive

Older builds that have rotated out of the live releases, kept on record here.

**Retention policy (2026-10-21):** the last 4 releases per product line stay
live on the [Releases page](https://github.com/waderaider/marigold/releases).
Older builds are documented on this page instead of being kept as downloads.
The downloadable assets of archived builds were removed under this policy,
but their source and history are preserved in this repo's git history and in
the per-version changelog files shipped with each build.
